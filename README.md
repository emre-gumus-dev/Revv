# 🏎️ Modu | The Ultimate Automotive Aftermarket Platform

**Modu** is a high-performance, niche product review and discovery platform dedicated to car modifications. Built with the latest web technologies, it helps enthusiasts find the perfect parts, verify fitment, and visualize their builds in a sleek "Dark Carbon Garage" environment.

> **Mission:** To bridge the gap between complex automotive engineering and passionate DIY mechanics through data-driven reviews and community-powered fitment data.

## 🚀 Tech Stack

*   **Framework:** [Next.js 16](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router & Server Components)
*   **Language:** [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/) (Strict Mode)
*   **Database:** [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/) with [Prisma ORM](https://www.prisma.io/)
*   **Styling:** [Tailwind CSS 4](https://tailwindcss.com/) + [Radix UI](https://www.radix-ui.com/) Primitives
*   **Authentication:** [NextAuth.js v5](https://authjs.dev/) (Pluggable providers)
*   **Internationalization:** [next-intl](https://next-intl-docs.vercel.app/) (EN, TR, DE support)
*   **Icons:** [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)

## ✨ Key Features

### 🛠️ Intelligent Vehicle Selector
A persistent, multi-step dropdown (Year > Make > Model > Trim) that filters the entire site's content to show only compatible parts for the user's specific chassis.

### 🌑 Dark Carbon Aesthetic
A premium, industrial UI featuring deep charcoal backgrounds, subtle carbon fiber textures, and neon accent colors inspired by JDM and Euro tuning cultures.

### 📊 Programmatic SEO (pSEO) Engine
Automated generation of thousands of niche landing pages (e.g., `/vehicles/bmw/e46/best-coilovers`) to capture long-tail search intent and drive organic traffic.

### 🔊 Audio-First Reviews
Integrated audio players for exhaust and intake reviews, allowing users to listen to "Cold Start," "Idle," and "RPM Pull" sounds directly on the product page.

### 🏗️ Virtual Garage & Build Planner
Users can save parts to their profile, calculate total build costs, estimate HP gains, and share their "Stage 1/2/3" builds with the community.
